Action-conditioned video world model · end-to-end

FlockDiT pipeline

From a boid simulator to long multi-agent video: simulate flocking, compress frames with a VAE, train a flow-matching transformer to predict future frames from context + steering actions, then generate long videos by autoregressive rollout. Ten camera-agents are modeled jointly so cross-agent attention can keep their overlapping views consistent.

What we want: when two agents can see the same boid, their views should place it in the same spot. The ceiling is the best that's achievable (measured on the real video); the floor is what 10 separate models that never share information would score. We want the model up near the ceiling — that would mean the agents are genuinely using each other's views.
